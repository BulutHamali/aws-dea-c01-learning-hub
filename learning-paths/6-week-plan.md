# Six-week study plan for people working full-time

[Home](../README.md) · [Four-week plan](4-week-plan.md) · [Progress tracker](../CHECKLIST.md) · [Study method](study-method.md)

Three 45-minute weekday sessions plus one longer weekend session of about 90 minutes. Weekday sessions cover one or two guides. Weekend sessions are for a lab or practice questions. Take the [diagnostic](../practice/diagnostic.md) before week 1.

| Week | Weekday 1 | Weekday 2 | Weekday 3 | Weekend |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · Streams and movement | [Streaming](../topic-guides/streaming.md) | [Lambda](../topic-guides/lambda.md) | [Data movement](../topic-guides/data-movement.md), [APIs and SDKs](../topic-guides/apis-and-sdks.md) | [Lab 2: streaming](../labs/hands-on/02-streaming/README.md) or the [pipeline challenges](../practice/pipeline-challenges.md) |
| 2 · Processing and orchestration | [Glue](../topic-guides/glue.md) | [EMR](../topic-guides/emr.md), [Programming concepts](../topic-guides/pipeline-engineering.md) | [Orchestration](../topic-guides/orchestration.md), [Deployment](../topic-guides/deployment.md) | [Lab 1: batch lake](../labs/hands-on/01-batch-lake/README.md), then Domain 1 questions in the [question bank](../practice/question-bank.md) |
| 3 · Storage | [S3](../topic-guides/s3.md) | [Databases](../topic-guides/databases.md), [File layout](../topic-guides/file-layout.md) | [Catalog](../topic-guides/catalog.md), [Iceberg](../topic-guides/iceberg.md) | [Redshift](../topic-guides/redshift.md), then [lab 3](../labs/hands-on/03-redshift-load/README.md) |
| 4 · Models and operations | [Data modeling](../topic-guides/data-modeling.md), [Vectors and LLMs](../topic-guides/vectors-and-llms.md) | [Athena](../topic-guides/athena.md), [SQL patterns](../topic-guides/sql-patterns.md) | [Monitoring](../topic-guides/monitoring.md) | [Data quality](../topic-guides/data-quality.md), [Analysis and prep](../topic-guides/analysis-and-data-prep.md), then the [timed set](../practice/timed-set.md) |
| 5 · Security and governance | [Security](../topic-guides/security.md) | [Networking](../topic-guides/networking.md) | [Governance](../topic-guides/governance.md), [Privacy and audit](../topic-guides/privacy-and-audit.md) | [Unified Studio](../topic-guides/catalog-and-unified-studio.md), [Service recognition](../topic-guides/service-recognition.md), then [labs 4 and 5](../labs/README.md#hands-on-labs) |
| 6 · Exam readiness | Review your error log | [Recall cards](../practice/recall-cards.md) for weak domains | Question bank items for weak skills | [Full mock](../practice/full-mock.md) under exam timing, then AWS's official practice exam |

## How to use the error log

Record every missed or guessed question in the [progress tracker](../CHECKLIST.md) with the deciding requirement you overlooked. Retry it two days later and again a week later. The [exam map](../EXAM-MAP.md#skill-by-skill-map) links each skill to its teaching section and to other questions on the same skill.
