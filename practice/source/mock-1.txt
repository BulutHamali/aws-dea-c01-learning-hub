stem: Analysts in Redshift run daily reports that join a 3-billion-row read-count fact table to a 50-row instrument table and a 20-million-row sample table, always joined on sample_id. Which table design reduces data movement during these joins?
A: DISTSTYLE EVEN on all three tables, with a compound sort key on the fact table's date || EVEN spreads rows round-robin, so large joins redistribute data on every query.

explain: Distribute large tables on their common join key. Small dimensions can go to every node.
